About me (more details)

Yara Alnamlah is a Saudi entrepreneur, beauty expert, and influencer born in 1999. She rose to fame on social media in 2016 through makeup and skincare content, and was listed in Forbes’ “30 Under 30” for 2024. She graduated from Prince Sultan University with a degree in Architecture in 2022. She is the founder and CEO of several beauty and lifestyle ventures, including the holding company When Minds Meet (WMM) and brands such as Moonglaze, Treat Salon, Tinka, and SoMatcha. She has also collaborated with global brands like Dior, Chanel, and Hermès, and is known for her soft, natural makeup style that emphasizes simple beauty.

Featured Work

Treat Products - 2023

Treat is a beauty brand based on the idea that beauty starts from within and self-care. Its products are designed for simple daily rituals that support wellbeing while being mindful of the environment.

Moonglaze - 2024

The brand is inspired by Arab culture, where beauty is openly celebrated. It takes from the word “gamar”, meaning “the moon” often used to describe someone as very beautiful, symbolising perfection and radiance like a full moon.

Sephora X Moonglaze - 2026

“Moonglaze” the Saudi beauty brand founded by Yara Alnamlah, was officially launched at Sephora Middle East on February 6, 2026, becoming the first Saudi local brand to secure shelf space at Sephora.


Treat Salon - 2023

Treat Salon is a beauty salon offering high-quality hair, skin, and nail services in a modern and relaxing environment, reflecting professionalism and attention to detail.

SoMatcha - 2021

So Matcha is a shop offering high-quality matcha drinks and products, focusing on authentic Japanese-inspired flavor within a modern and elegant experience that reflects attention to detail.

Tinka Cosmetics - 2022

Tinka Cosmetics is a Saudi brand specializing in nail polish, focusing on healthier and less harmful formulas for nails. It offers modern products that care for nails while maintaining an elegant look.
